# Brief Specifications

## **PCB:**
- Flexcut  
- Hotswap  
- 32 keys with support for 2 encoders  
- Coin battery or lithium battery  
- **MCU:** Nice Nano V2.0  

## **Case:**
- Gasket mount  
- Two-part, easy to print  
- 7% tilt  
- Wrist rest  

> *(If you’re wondering why the wrist rest has a hole, it’s designed to function as a pen rest.)*  

**⚠️ Note:** The switch for powering on and off hasn’t been completed yet, which is why there’s just a hole there for now. That’s one of the things that still needs to be finished.

---

## **Required Parts:**

### **1. Gasket Tape:**
- **Thickness:** 3 mm  
- Used to dampen vibrations and achieve better sound and feel while typing.  
- You can stick the gasket tape directly onto the plate or the case—it’s up to you.  

> *(I prefer sticking it onto the plate since I often change cases, but the plate stays the same.)*  

### **2. Screws:**
- **Number of screws:** 7 pieces  
- **Dimensions:** 3 mm x 20 mm *(slightly shorter screws are also fine)*  
- **Recommendation:** Use the screws from the link below, as they have been tested:  
  [AliExpress Link](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/4001120507552.html?spm=a2g0o.order_list.order_list_main.13.6ca9180263b2qE)

### **3. haredware:**
- NiceNano v2
- On-off switch, MSK-12C02 7 pin 

#### **🔹 Note about screws:**
- **Directly into plastic:** Screws can be inserted directly into plastic parts, but this might reduce the durability of the joints.  
- **Durability tips:** It is recommended to use inserts for 3D-printed parts to increase strength and longevity.  
- **Recommended inserts:** Use brass or steel inserts compatible with plastic parts.  

---

### **Important Note:**
📌 **Approximately 400 grams of filament** will be needed for the entire casing, including the plate.
